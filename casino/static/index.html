Cassino

How to play

Goal: collect cards from the table to win points. You play one deal against the computer.

Cards

Ace = 1, 2–10 are their number, Jack = 11, Queen = 12, King = 13. Each player holds 3 cards; 4 lie on the table. New cards are dealt when both hands are empty (7 rounds).

Your turn: do one of these

  1. Take: click a card in your hand, then the table cards it takes, then press Take. It takes a card of the same value (5 takes a 5), or cards that add up to it (9 takes 4+5), or several groups at once (7 takes a 7 and 3+4). Dashed outlines show what your selected card can take.
  2. Place: click one hand card and press Place to put it on the table.

You may also select two hand cards: their sum counts as one value (5+A takes 4+2).

Sweep

Taking every card on the table is a sweep: 1 point, and the other player must then play twice in a row.

Points at the end

  • 3 — 27 or more cards
  • 2 — 7 or more spades
  • 1 each — aces
  • 2 — 10♦
  • 1 — 2♠
  • 1 each — sweeps

Whoever took cards last also gets what is left on the table when the deal ends.
